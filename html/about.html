ABOUT

"Big Buddy Tutoring Program is a community-focused initiative designed to support elementary school students through academic help, mentorship, and positive role modeling. By pairing high-achieving high school volunteers with younger learners, the program creates meaningful one-on-one connections that encourage confidence, growth, and a love of learning. Working in partnership with local schools and libraries, Big Buddy provides a safe and supportive environment for students who may be struggling academically, socially, or emotionally. Our goal is to strengthen educational outcomes while inspiring young leaders to give back and make a lasting impact in their community. "

Author: Simone Urie
